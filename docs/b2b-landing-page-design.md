```

Smaller teams benefit even more from Lego Serious Play because:

• Changes in collaboration happen faster with fewer people
• Workshop customization is easier and more targeted
• ROI per person is significantly higher

We've worked with teams as small as 5 and as large as 20. If your team 
is larger, we can discuss multi-session formats.
```

**Q2: "What's the time commitment? We can't afford to lose a whole day."**

**Answer:**
```
We offer two formats:

**Half-Day Workshop (4 hours):** Perfect for focused problem-solving or 
addressing a specific challenge. Most small businesses start here.

**Full-Day Workshop (7 hours):** Ideal for strategic planning, vision 
alignment, or complex challenges requiring deep exploration.

Both formats deliver actionable results. You'll lose more time in 
unproductive meetings over the next month than you'll invest in a 
Brick Spark workshop.
```

**Q3: "How quickly will we see results?"**

**Answer:**
```
Implementation starts the same week—often the very next day.

You'll walk out of the workshop with:

• Clear deadlines (typically 7-30 days)
• Documented action plan ready to execute

Most teams report noticeable improvements in communication and collaboration 
within the first week. Measurable business results (time saved, decisions made 
faster, problems solved) typically appear within 30-60 days.
```

```
This is the most common concern—and it disappears within 15 minutes of starting.

• Hands-on building neutralizes cynicism and defensiveness
• People stop thinking about Lego and start solving real problems

We've worked with engineering teams, finance departments, construction companies, 
and law firms. Skepticism is normal. Conversion is inevitable.

One CEO told us: "I thought this would be a waste of time. It was the most 
productive four hours we've had as a team in years."
```

## IMPLEMENTATION GUIDE & CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION TIPS

### Page Structure Summary

The landing page follows a proven conversion path:

1. **Hook** (Hero) → Grab attention with the core problem
2. **Agitate** (Problem Section) → Make the pain real and urgent
3. **Solution** (Mechanism) → Introduce Lego Serious Play as the answer
4. **Value** (Benefits) → Show specific, measurable outcomes
5. **Process** (How It Works) → Remove mystery and reduce friction
6. **Proof** (Logos + Testimonials) → Build credibility and trust
7. **Stack** (Value Section) → Demonstrate overwhelming value
8. **Address Objections** (FAQ) → Remove final barriers
9. **Convert** (Final CTA) → Drive to qualification questionnaire

### Conversion Optimization Principles Applied

#### Alex Hormozi's Value Equation:
- ✓ **Dream Outcome:** Clear outcomes (better collaboration, actionable tasks)
- ✓ **Perceived Likelihood:** Social proof, 11,000+ participants, specific results
- ✓ **Time Delay:** Immediate results emphasized ("walk out with action plan")
- ✓ **Effort & Sacrifice:** Minimal (half-day option, no long commitment)

#### Russell Brunson's Story Framework:
- ✓ **Hook:** "Your team meetings don't produce results"
- ✓ **Story:** Transformation narratives in testimonials
- ✓ **Offer:** Value stack with clear deliverables
- ✓ **Urgency:** Subtle (cost of inaction, limited availability implied)

#### Daniel Priestley's Qualification:
- ✓ **Dream outcomes** identified in questionnaire
- ✓ **Previous solutions** explored to understand sophistication
- ✓ **Budget parameters** established early
- ✓ **Timeline** determined for prioritization
- ✓ **Decision authority** clarified to avoid wasted conversations

### Key Design Elements

#### Color Psychology:
- **Navy Blue:** Trust, professionalism, stability
- **Orange:** Energy, action, creativity (CTAs)
- **White/Cream:** Clarity, openness, simplicity

#### CTA Button Best Practices:
- Use action-oriented text ("Start," "Get," "See")
- Repeat CTAs every 2-3 sections (at least 5-6 throughout page)

- Always link to qualification questionnaire (single conversion goal)

#### Mobile Optimization:

### Brand Voice Guidelines

#### Tone Characteristics:
- **Direct** - Don't sugarcoat problems or oversell solutions
- **Conversational** - Write like you're talking to a fellow business owner
- **Confident** - You know this works, but you're not arrogant about it
- **Honest** - If you're not a fit, say so
- **Slightly Provocative** - Challenge assumptions, but stay friendly
- **Results-Focused** - Always tie back to business outcomes

#### Words to Use:
- Actionable, concrete, immediate, specific, measurable
- Transform, solve, improve, deliver, implement
- Team, collaboration, decisions, results, ROI

#### Words to Avoid:
- Synergy, paradigm, leverage, disrupt, revolutionary
- Amazing, incredible, awesome, unbelievable
- Game-changer (unless in a testimonial)
- Best-in-class, world-class, cutting-edge
